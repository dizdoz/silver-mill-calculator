The ring size describes the INSIDE of the finished ring. Silver thickness therefore matters: the strip length follows the centre line, so approximately π × thickness is added to the internal circumference. Your join/sizing allowance is then added on top. UK sizes and circumferences are based on the Cooksongold ring-sizing chart. This is a starting calculation; your exact forming method may require a different allowance.
